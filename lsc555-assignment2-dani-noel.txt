		Looking through the search engine on the Tower, I searched using simply “LGBT” as my topic. As expected of an institution connected to the Catholic Church, Catholic University has a very tenuous relationship with acceptance of LGBT individuals. While officially tolerant, CUA board officially denied any LGBT association repeatedly, until CUAllies was recognized in 2021. For teachers of religion, social studies, psychology, or medicine, it is relevant to look at the many articles discussing LGBT individuals in terms of medical science, Church acceptance (or lack thereof) and doctrine, as well as social situations.
